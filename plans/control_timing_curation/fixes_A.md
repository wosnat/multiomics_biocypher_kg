Not applied / needs the curator:
- **S12–S14 identity (800 ppm).** The evidence (script, anticorrelation with S9–S11) says these are most likely contrasts 7–9: the partner-specific pCO2 interaction minus the mean of the three. That differs from the paper's own presentation (deviation from the general coculture response at 800 ppm). I labelled them as the paper presents them and added a caveat. The curator may prefer to rename them as partner × pCO2 interaction deviations, or to drop them. A definitive answer needs the authors' code that wrote S12–S14, or re-running the GLM. R is not installed here; `ALL_EZ55_2.Rdata` is available.
- **The 6 partner experiments keep `treatment_type: [coculture]` and a single `treatment_organism`.** Their control is the mean over all three partners, so the other two partners are present in the control as well. They were not added as `background_organisms` because that was not on the item list. Coculture-vs-axenic `partner_role` semantics do not fit these contrasts well.
- **The cyanobacterial pCO2 experiments (MIT9312, CC9311, WH8102) say "axenic and coculture conditions" and list `background_factors: [axenic, coculture]`.** The cyanobacteria were only sequenced in coculture (p. 3: "We did not observe the growth of axenic Synechococcus strains for this study"; the cyanobacterial scripts fit `~Treatment` on the coculture libraries). This was not on my list, so it is unchanged. Suggest `[coculture, diel]`.
- **`diel` was not added to the 5 non-coculture experiments.** These are the 3 cyanobacterial ones, S2 and S4, none of which listed `light`. The instruction was to replace `light` where listed. Adding `diel` would be consistent with Hennon 2017 (`[axenic, diel]`).
