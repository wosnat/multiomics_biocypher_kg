Data S1. (separate file)
Convergently evolved genes in phytoplankton genomes. “PCO2 Search” indicates whether the
given gene passed our curation criteria in one or both of the pCO2 treatments, which were
analyzed separately; “Total Mutations” indicates the total number of unique mutations observed
across all lineages at the indicated pCO2 treatment; “Lineages Represented” indicates the number
of replicately evolved lineages in which at least one mutation was observed for the given gene;
“P Value” is the significance level of the comparison between total mutations observed in each
pCO2 treatment.

Data S2. (separate file)
Convergently evolved genes in Alteromonas genomes, binned by phytoplankton partner and
pCO2 treatment and curated by pCO2 treatment. “PCO2 Search” indicates whether the given
gene passed our curation criteria in one or both of the pCO2 treatments, which were analyzed
separately in each phytoplankton partner treatment; “Total Mutations” indicates the total number
of unique mutations observed across all lineages at the indicated partner and pCO2 treatment;
“Lineages Represented” indicates the number of replicately evolved lineages in which at least
one mutation was observed for the given gene; “P Value” is the significance level of the
comparison between total mutations observed in each pCO2 treatment.

Data S3. (separate file)
Convergently evolved genes in Alteromonas genomes, binned by phytoplankton partner and
pCO2 treatment and curated by phytoplankton partner. “Significant In” indicates with which
phytoplankton partner(s) the gene passed our curation criteria; “Total Mutations” indicates the
total number of unique mutations observed across all lineages for the indicated partner;
“Lineages Represented” indicates the number of replicately evolved lineages in which at least
one mutation was observed for the given gene; “P Values” indicate the significance level of the
comparison between total mutations observed between the indicated phytoplankton pairings.
