# Anjur-Dietrich 2026

**Citation:** Anjur-Dietrich MI, Jones KG, Mullet JI, Vo NN, Castro KG, Parker SM, Chisholm SW. "Biofilm formation and dynamics in the marine cyanobacterium *Prochlorococcus*." *Nature Communications* (2026).
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-026-77598-7
**Organism(s):** *Prochlorococcus* MIT9301 (RNA-seq); the biofilm phenotype is characterised across MED4, MIT9301, MIT9312, NATL2A, MIT9211, MIT1205 and MIT9313 (physiology only, not per-gene).
**Topic:** *Prochlorococcus* populations transition reversibly between planktonic and biofilm states. Planktonic and biofilm enrichment populations (each serially selected for >100 generations) of axenic MIT9301 were grown undisturbed to mid-exponential phase and compared by RNA-seq (edgeR, |logFC| > 0.8, q < 0.05): 97 genes more expressed in biofilm cells, 145 in planktonic cells. RNA-seq reads: SRA PRJNA1481284.
